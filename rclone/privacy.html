Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

rclone Drive Sync ("the app") is a private application used only by its owner, Veysel Karani Tekgöz, to synchronize files with his own Google Drive.

Data accessed

With the user's consent through Google OAuth, the app reads, uploads, updates and deletes files in the owner's own Google Drive via the Google Drive API.

Use of data

Data is used only to keep the owner's local folders and Google Drive in sync. It is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Storage

Files and OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own devices. No data is collected from other users.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
